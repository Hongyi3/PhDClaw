# paper-ingest

## Purpose

Ingest literature identifiers and local artifacts into a verified corpus.

## Inputs

Define concrete typed inputs before implementation. Prefer identifiers, manifests, and local files over free-form prose.

## Outputs

- normalized corpus manifest
- verified citation records
- ingestion log

## Rules

- do not invent references
- preserve provenance
- label uncertainty explicitly
- write machine-readable artifacts where practical
